Why BioScan?

BioScan is a personal educational project designed to explore the connection between biomedical engineering and software development.


The project combines medical imaging concepts, interactive web technologies and simplified visualization to demonstrate how engineering can transform complex technical concepts into accessible digital experiences.

⚠️ BioScan is an educational project. It does not provide medical diagnosis or clinical recommendations.
